How to Live
Through Emotions

A space to slow down, notice what is happening inside, and allow yourself to feel.

Begin
01

You don't have to understand everything you feel.

Sometimes we try to explain our emotions before we have even allowed ourselves to experience them.

We push them away. We make ourselves busy. We become strong. We keep going.

This journey begins somewhere else. With attention. With sensation. With permission to feel.

02

This space is for women.

For women who feel too much.

For women who feel nothing.

For women who are tired of holding everything together.

For women who want to come a little closer to themselves.

03

The Journey

There is nothing to achieve here. Move through the practices at your own pace.

01
Arrive

Come back to yourself.

Before we can feel, we need a moment to arrive. Notice your body. Your breath. The space around you.

02
Notice

What is here?

You don't need to name the feeling perfectly. Just notice what your body is telling you.

03
Feel

Allow the feeling to exist.

You don't have to fight it. You don't have to become it. You can simply stay with it.

04
Return

Come back to life.

Feelings move. You don't have to hold on to them forever.

01

Arriving

Begin by slowing down. Give yourself permission to be here exactly as you are.

What do you notice in your body right now?

02

Feeling

Stay close to what is happening inside you. There is no right way to feel.

If this feeling had a shape, temperature or movement, what would it be?

03

Letting go

You are allowed to let a feeling move through you. You don't have to carry everything with you.

What feels different now?

✧

Nothing needs to happen right now.

Take a breath. Look around you. Let this moment be enough.

04

You don't have to fix anything.

You were here. You noticed. You felt.

And that is enough.
