A little guidance

There is no right way to feel.

You don't have to complete everything at once. You don't have to force yourself to feel anything.

Find a quiet moment. Watch one video. Notice what happens inside you. Give yourself time.

You can pause. You can come back later. Your experience does not need to look like anyone else's.
