Підтримати
To Stay Alive

Emotional detox

A movement-based course for processing emotions

Begin
BEFORE YOU BEGIN

Start with yourself

Watch this short introduction before you begin. Vera will guide you through the space and explain how to work with the practices.

01

You don't have
to fix anything.

Emotions don't need to be defeated, suppressed, or made convenient.

You can notice them. Feel them. Let them be.

HOW IT WORKS

Simply be here.

Choose the emotion that feels closest to you right now. You don't have to go through everything at once.

01
Pause

Give yourself a few minutes.

Put away unnecessary notifications. Sit or lie down in whatever way feels comfortable.

02
Feel

Notice what is here.

You don't need to understand where the emotion came from right away.

03
Stay

Give yourself some time.

You can pause the video, return later, or simply stay in silence.

01 · EMOTION

Fear

Stay with this emotion for a while. You don't have to change it.

What do you notice in yourself right now?

02 · EMOTION

Anger

Allow yourself to notice what lies beneath this energy.

What does your anger want to say?

03 · EMOTION

Shame

Stay with yourself without judgment. You don't have to be perfect here.

What is happening inside you?

04 · EMOTION

Grief

Sometimes there is nothing you need to do. You can simply stay close to what hurts.

What would you like to allow yourself to feel?

05 · EMOTION

Joy

Joy can be experienced fully too. You don't have to rush away from it.

Where do you feel joy in your body?

♡

to happen right now.

Take a pause. Drink some water. Look out the window. Stay with yourself.

TO FINISH

You can
come back here.

Not only when everything is fine. But whenever you need a little more space for yourself.

Take care of yourself.
